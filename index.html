About EduRadar

Your live radar for AI, education, skills, and the future of work.

What is EduRadar?

EduRadar is a free, open, automated intelligence feed that aggregates news, research, policy, and publications from over 150 sources worldwide — all focused on the intersection of AI with education, skills, TVET, and the labor market.

It is updated every 6 hours by a Python script that reads RSS and Atom feeds from multilaterals, bilateral donors, foundations, NGOs, universities, think tanks, academic journals, major media houses, and regional press. Every story links to its original source. No paywalls. No tracking. No ads.

Why EduRadar?

There is no single platform that brings together multilateral research, regional education press from the Global South, academic journals, AI lab announcements, policy updates, and funding opportunities — all in one place, free, live, and searchable. EduRadar fills that gap.

It is designed to be useful for education policymakers, researchers, teachers, funders, EdTech entrepreneurs, and anyone working at the intersection of learning and the future of work.

How it works

A Python script reads all sources in sources.yaml every 6 hours using GitHub Actions. Each story is classified into one of 12 streams using keyword rules. AI lab and general media stories pass through an education gate — only stories that mention education, learning, or skills are kept. Everything is stored in SQLite and published as a static JSON file that this page reads directly.

Robots.txt is respected on every fetch. Summaries are the publishers' own text, truncated to 600 characters. No content is reproduced in full.
